# Troubleshoot Attachment Problems

## A confidential attachment is public

1. Select **Switch to private**.
2. Save and confirm the file moved to Private Files.
3. Sign out and confirm it is absent publicly.
4. Follow district policy if confidential material was exposed.

## A public attachment is private

Confirm public-release approval, select **Switch to public**, save, and verify the signed-out view.

## The old version displays

Remove the old attachment, upload the approved replacement with a new filename, confirm its description and Display setting, save, and open it.

## An attachment does not appear

Confirm its Agenda Item, access area, Display setting, saved state, and—when private—the viewer's sign-in and Group membership.

## A section was duplicated with attachments

Duplicating a section duplicates all its attachments. Remove the duplicated attachments first, then remove the unintended duplicate section and save.

Do not duplicate a populated section merely to reuse its structure.

## A section must be deleted

Confirm the target, remove its attachments first, remove the section, then save and review the draft.
