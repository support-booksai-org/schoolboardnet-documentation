# Publishing, Notifications, and Revisions

Publication is the final step, not part of routine drafting. Keep the agenda **Private**, leave **Notifications** unchecked, and leave **Published** unchecked until review is complete.

There is no separate publication screen. After the agenda is saved with **Published** selected, schoolboard.net displays the saved agenda.

## Final review before publication

Before changing any publication setting, confirm:

- the meeting title, date, time, and Agenda Header are correct;
- the correct Group is actively selected;
- all template placeholders and unused blank sections were removed;
- section order and Agenda Item content are final;
- public and private attachments are correctly classified;
- replacement files open correctly;
- notification recipients and timing are approved; and
- meaningful Revision Text is ready when a revision notice will be sent.

!!! warning "Do not publish from memory"
    Save the agenda in Draft Mode and review the saved result. Do not rely on Preview or unsaved changes when deciding that an agenda is ready.

## Publish the agenda and notify the public

When the approved agenda is ready for public release:

1. Edit the saved draft.
2. Change **Visibility** to **Public**.
3. Select **Notifications** when the public notice should be sent.
4. Select **Published**.
5. Confirm the correct Group.
6. Save.

After saving, the agenda display opens. The public notification links subscribers to the updated agenda.

!!! important "Notifications are sent when saved"
    Confirm the agenda, audience, and timing before saving with **Notifications** selected. Do not select Notifications merely as a reminder for later.

## Verify the published agenda

The saved agenda appears after publication.

![Published Accordion Agenda with collapsible sections and print controls.](assets/images/SB-AAC-020-published-agenda.png){ .doc-screenshot }

*Figure SB-AAC-020. The published agenda display.*

1. Sign out of the administrator account.
2. Open the Group's public page.
3. Open the meeting agenda.
4. Expand every changed section.
5. Open each public file and public Expandable HTML item.
6. Confirm that no private item is visible.
7. Test **Print Full Packet** and **Print Agenda Only** when printing is part of the district's review procedure.

!!! important "Preview is not the public view"
    Preview shows the administrator view. Only a signed-out check verifies what an anonymous public visitor can access.

## Confirm public notification delivery

Group Administrators should subscribe to the Group's public notifications using a personal or otherwise independent email address.

Receiving the notice confirms that:

- the public notification was distributed;
- its title and formatting are understandable;
- the agenda link works; and
- the public agenda can be reached outside the administrator account.

If the independent account does not receive the expected notice, verify the publication and notification settings before assuming subscribers received it.

## Revise a published agenda

Use revisions to preserve a change record and explain updates after an agenda has been published.

1. Open the current published agenda for editing.
2. Make the approved changes.
3. Select **Create new revision**.
4. Enter meaningful **Revision Text** describing what changed.
5. Confirm the intended visibility, notification audience, and publication setting.
6. Save.
7. Reopen and verify the newest saved revision.

Good Revision Text is brief and specific, for example:

- **Section 5: Minutes updated**
- **Section 11: Administrative Reports added**
- **Building Committee attachment replaced**

Avoid vague entries such as **updated**, **changes**, or **revised agenda**.

![Group-member revision notification showing Revision Text.](assets/images/SB-AAC-007-notification-group-members.png){ .doc-screenshot }

*Figure SB-AAC-007. Meaningful Revision Text tells Group members what changed.*

The numbered callout identifies the content entered in **Revision Text**. That text is included in the Group-member notification so recipients can understand the change without comparing every section.

!!! tip "Use the newest revision"
    When revisions create several entries with the same title, use the **Updated** date and time to select the most recent revision. Do not delete older entries until the current published version has been confirmed.

## Notify Group members without notifying the public

When an approved revision should notify authorized Group members but should not send another public notice, use this controlled sequence:

1. Edit the current agenda and make the approved change.
2. Select **Create new revision** and enter meaningful Revision Text.
3. Set **Visibility** to **Private**.
4. Select **Notifications** and save to notify the assigned Group members.
5. Reopen the saved agenda.
6. Return **Visibility** to **Public**.
7. Leave **Notifications** unchecked and save again.
8. Sign out and verify that the agenda is public.

!!! warning "Do not leave the agenda Private"
    The Group-only notification step temporarily makes the agenda Private. Complete the second save that restores **Public** visibility, then verify the signed-out public view.

## Notification and revision checklist

Before finishing, confirm:

- the agenda is **Published**;
- final **Visibility** is **Public** when the agenda should be public;
- Notifications were selected only for the intended save and audience;
- Revision Text accurately explains the change;
- the newest revision is the one being reviewed;
- the independent public-notification account received the expected notice; and
- the signed-out agenda and public links work correctly.
