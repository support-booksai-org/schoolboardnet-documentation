# Best Practices and Final Checklist

Use these practices to keep agenda preparation consistent, secure, and easy to review.

## Assign one owner for each meeting

Designate one administrator to maintain the agenda structure and coordinate the meeting through publication. Other Group Administrators may add approved content and attachments, but simultaneous structural editing should be avoided.

This provides a clear source of truth and reduces conflicting or accidental changes.

## Clone an approved template

- Clone the template for the correct meeting type.
- Never convert the master template into a current meeting.
- Maintain separate templates for recurring formats such as Regular Meeting, Regular Meeting with Closed Session, Work Session, and Special Meeting.
- Include blank recurring sections and Expandable HTML components in templates.
- Complete the blank sections needed for the meeting and remove unused blank sections before publication.
- Change master templates only when the district intentionally changes its standard structure.

!!! tip "Need help building a template?"
    Send schoolboard.net Support an approved outline, Word document, or sample agenda. The “magical elves” can build a reusable template and help the district establish a consistent starting point.

## Work in Draft Mode

While preparing and reviewing an agenda, keep:

- **Visibility:** Private
- **Notifications:** Unchecked
- **Published:** Unchecked

Save frequently and review the saved draft. Preview is an administrator view and is not the recommended normal review method.

## Verify Group assignment

After cloning, actively select the intended Board or committee Group even when it appears highlighted. Group assignment affects where the agenda appears, who may access private material, and who may receive Group notifications.

## Protect public and private content

- Place confidential files and Expandable HTML under the private controls.
- Confirm authorization before using **Switch to public**.
- Open every replacement attachment after saving.
- Use a new filename for a replacement file so browsers and search indexes recognize the new version.
- Sign out after publication and verify the anonymous public view.

## Use meaningful Revision Text

Describe exactly what changed. Revision Text may appear in Group-member notifications and should let recipients understand the update without comparing the entire agenda.

## Confirm public notification delivery

Subscribe to the Group's public notifications using a personal or otherwise independent email address. Receiving the notice and opening its link provides an independent confirmation of delivery and public access.

## Ask before guessing

Contact the District Administrator or schoolboard.net Support whenever Group assignment, privacy, publication, notifications, revisions, or attachment access is uncertain. There are no stupid questions.

## Final agenda checklist

### Meeting information

- [ ] The title, date, time, and Agenda Header are correct.
- [ ] The correct Group was actively selected.
- [ ] All template placeholder text was replaced.
- [ ] Unused blank sections were removed.

### Agenda structure and content

- [ ] Sections appear in the approved order.
- [ ] Agenda Items belong beneath the correct sections.
- [ ] Public and private files belong beneath the intended Agenda Items.
- [ ] Public and private Expandable HTML is complete and correctly classified.
- [ ] Duplicate, obsolete, and unintended content was removed.

### Attachments

- [ ] Every attachment has a meaningful description.
- [ ] Every public attachment is approved for public release.
- [ ] Every confidential attachment is under **Private Files**.
- [ ] Replacement files use new filenames and open correctly.
- [ ] Display and attachment order are correct.

### Publication and notification

- [ ] The saved draft was reviewed before publication.
- [ ] **Visibility** is Public when the agenda should be publicly available.
- [ ] **Published** is selected.
- [ ] Notifications were selected only for the intended audience and save.
- [ ] Revision Text clearly explains a published update.
- [ ] The independent public-notification account received the expected notice.
- [ ] The signed-out public view and its links were verified.
