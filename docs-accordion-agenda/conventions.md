## Preferred and alternative methods

The **preferred method** is schoolboard.net's normal recommended workflow. An **alternative method** may be appropriate when the preferred method does not fit the meeting.

## Phones and tablets

1. Select **☰ Topics** to open the guide navigation.
2. Select a page.
3. Select the page-outline icon to open the page **Table of Contents**.
4. Select a heading to move directly to that section.

The current page title remains above the Table of Contents so its context is not lost while scrolling.
