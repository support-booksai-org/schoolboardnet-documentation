# Troubleshooting and Getting Help

Start with the symptom that most closely matches the problem. Make one controlled correction at a time, save, and verify the result before making another change.

## I cannot find an agenda or other content

1. Check **Drafts & Templates** on the Group home page.
2. Select **Show More** because the list may not display every item initially.
3. Remember that the list is sorted by meeting date in reverse order, newest first.
4. Verify that you are looking in the correct Group.
5. Ask a District Administrator to open the administrative **Content** list, which displays content across the site.
6. Search by title or scroll through the list.
7. Verify the agenda's **Group** assignment and publication status.

If revisions are enabled, several entries may have the same title. Use the **Updated** date and time to select the most recent revision.

!!! warning "Do not recreate it immediately"
    Before creating another agenda, confirm that the original does not already exist. Recreating content can produce duplicate agendas and conflicting versions.

## A template is missing from Drafts & Templates

- Select **Show More**.
- Look near the bottom of the list because permanent templates are often assigned intentionally older dates.
- Confirm that the template belongs to the correct Group.
- Ask the designated template administrator before creating a replacement.

## The cloned agenda is missing from its Group

The Group may have appeared highlighted without being selected when the clone was saved.

1. Ask a District Administrator to locate the agenda in **Content** if it cannot be reached from the Group page.
2. Edit the agenda.
3. Actively select the correct Group.
4. Save in Draft Mode.
5. Return to the Group home page and confirm that the draft appears.

## The public cannot see the agenda

Verify all of the following:

- **Visibility** is Public.
- **Published** is selected.
- The correct public-facing Group is assigned.
- The agenda was saved after the final settings were selected.

Then sign out and test the agenda from the Group's public page.

!!! warning "Check after a Group-only notification"
    The Group-only notification workflow temporarily changes Visibility to Private. Reopen the agenda, restore Public visibility with Notifications unchecked, save, and verify the signed-out view.

## Board or Group members cannot see private material

Check:

- the agenda is assigned to the correct Group;
- the user is a current member of that Group;
- the material is under the private controls of the intended Agenda Item; and
- the user is signed in with the correct account.

If membership is incorrect, contact the Group or District Administrator responsible for membership management.

## A confidential attachment is public

Act immediately.

1. Edit the affected Agenda Item.
2. Select **Switch to private** beside the file.
3. Save.
4. Reopen the item and confirm the file appears under **Private Files**.
5. Sign out and verify that the file is absent from the public view.
6. Notify the appropriate district contact according to district policy if the file was exposed publicly.

## A public attachment is private

1. Confirm that the file is approved for public release.
2. Select **Switch to public**.
3. Save.
4. Reopen the item and confirm the file appears under **Public Files**.
5. Verify it from the signed-out public view.

## An attachment displays the old version

1. Confirm that the approved replacement was uploaded.
2. Remove the old attachment.
3. Upload the replacement with a new filename.
4. Confirm its description and Display setting.
5. Save and reopen the file.

A new filename helps prevent a browser or search index from continuing to use the prior file.

## An attachment does not appear

- Confirm it was uploaded beneath the intended Agenda Item.
- Confirm it is in the correct public or private area.
- Confirm **Display** is selected.
- Save and reopen the Agenda Item.
- For private material, confirm the viewer is signed in and belongs to the assigned Group.

## A section was duplicated with attachments

Duplicating a section duplicates all attachments within it.

1. Identify the unintended duplicate section.
2. Remove its duplicated attachments first.
3. Remove the duplicate section.
4. Save and review the agenda.

Do not duplicate a populated section merely to reuse its structure. Create a new section, or duplicate only a confirmed attachment-free section.

## A section must be deleted

Removing a section deletes all of its nested content and attachments. Use the recommended sequence:

1. Confirm that the correct section was selected.
2. Remove its attachments first.
3. Remove the section.
4. Save and review the draft.

## A notification did not arrive

Check:

- Notifications was selected for the intended save;
- the correct Visibility and Group were selected for the intended audience;
- the agenda was saved successfully;
- the recipient is subscribed to the correct public notifications or is an authorized Group member, as applicable; and
- the message is not in spam or quarantine.

Use the independent public-notification account as the first delivery check. If delivery remains uncertain, contact schoolboard.net Support with the agenda title, Group, save time, intended audience, and the address that did not receive the notice.

## A notification contained an unclear update message

Create meaningful Revision Text on future revisions. Identify the section or material changed, such as **Section 11: Administrative Reports added** rather than **updated**.

## Several revisions have the same title

This is normal when revisions are enabled.

1. Review the **Updated** date and time.
2. Select the newest revision.
3. Confirm its publication status and content before editing.
4. Do not delete older entries until the current published version is confirmed.

## When to contact schoolboard.net Support

Contact Support when:

- content cannot be located after the District Administrator checks **Content**;
- notification delivery remains uncertain after the checklist is completed;
- access or Group behavior does not match the documented settings;
- a confidential-access issue requires assistance;
- a master template should be created or substantially revised; or
- an administrator is unsure how to correct a problem safely.

Include the district, Group, agenda title, relevant date and time, expected result, actual result, and screenshots when available. Security-related administrator requests must be submitted by email so there is a written record.
