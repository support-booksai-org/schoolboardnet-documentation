# Troubleshooting and Getting Help

Choose the page that most closely matches the problem. Make one controlled correction at a time, save, and verify the result before making another change.

## Choose a problem area

- [Find Content and Resolve Access](troubleshooting-find-access.md) — missing agendas or templates, incorrect Group assignment, public visibility, and private access.
- [Troubleshoot Attachment Problems](troubleshooting-attachments.md) — incorrect access, stale or missing files, duplicated attachments, and safe deletion.
- [Troubleshoot Notifications and Revisions](troubleshooting-notifications.md) — missing notices, unclear Revision Text, multiple revisions, and contacting Support.

## Spelling suggestions do not appear

Spell checking is supplied by the browser and operating system, not by a separate Accordion Agenda control. Follow [Browser Spell Check and Proofreading](https://docs.schoolboard.net/authoring/browser-spell-check/) to activate it in Chrome, Edge, Safari, iPad or iPhone and resolve common problems.

For prevention, review [Best Practices and Final Checklist](best-practices.md).
