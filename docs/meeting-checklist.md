# Meeting checklist

## Before the meeting

- [ ] Open the district site from a current bookmark or trusted link.
- [ ] Sign in with your own Board Member account.
- [ ] Confirm the meeting title, date, and time.
- [ ] Review notices and attendance or accommodation information.
- [ ] Expand and review each assigned agenda section.
- [ ] Open public supporting materials.
- [ ] Open authorized private materials.
- [ ] Search for subjects requiring special attention.
- [ ] Refresh the live agenda shortly before the meeting.

## After the meeting

- [ ] Sign out on a shared device.
- [ ] Secure or destroy printed private materials according to district policy.
- [ ] Report broken links, missing materials, or accessibility barriers.
- [ ] Avoid redistributing saved copies when the live agenda may have changed.
