# Final Authoring Checklist

## Source approval

- [ ] The current approved source was used.
- [ ] Comments, suggestions, tracked changes, and placeholders were resolved.
- [ ] Confidential content was separated from public content.

## Structure

- [ ] The title and headings are descriptive.
- [ ] Heading levels follow a logical order.
- [ ] Numbered and bulleted content uses real lists.
- [ ] Important meaning does not depend only on color, size, position, or bold text.

## Links, tables, and images

- [ ] Link text describes the destination.
- [ ] Every link opens the intended resource.
- [ ] Tables are necessary, simple, and have headers.
- [ ] Tables work on a narrow screen and at increased zoom.
- [ ] Informative images have useful alternative text.
- [ ] Important chart conclusions are also available as text.

## Formatting cleanup

- [ ] Unnecessary Word or Google Docs styling was removed.
- [ ] Tabs, repeated spaces, and empty paragraphs are not used for layout.
- [ ] Fonts and spacing are consistent with the schoolboard.net site.
- [ ] No print-only headers, footers, page numbers, or drafting notes remain.

## AI-assisted conversion

- [ ] The district approved the AI service and the type of content submitted.
- [ ] No confidential, private, student, personnel, attorney-client, or otherwise restricted information was submitted without authorization.
- [ ] The AI-generated HTML was compared with the complete approved source.
- [ ] Names, dates, numbers, quotations, legal language, and OCR results are exact.
- [ ] Invented content, prompts, review notes, and unnecessary markup were removed.
- [ ] Images, tables, reading order, and alternative text received human review.
- [ ] Only the intended `<div>` content was copied into the Expandable HTML Source editor.
- [ ] The normal editor and saved agenda were checked for content removed or changed during HTML cleanup.

## Publication

- [ ] Essential information is available as Expandable HTML whenever practical.
- [ ] Supplemental attachments are accessible and meaningfully named.
- [ ] Public and private placement is correct.
- [ ] The saved Draft Mode result was reviewed.
- [ ] The content was checked on desktop and mobile.
- [ ] A second authorized reviewer checked sensitive or complex material when required.
