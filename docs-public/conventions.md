# Conventions Used in This Guide

This page explains the visual and navigation conventions used throughout the Public User Guide.

## Information boxes

!!! tip "Tip"
    A suggestion that may make information easier to find or use.

!!! note "Note"
    Helpful background information or a related detail.

!!! important "Important"
    Information that deserves particular attention before continuing.

!!! warning "Warning"
    A potential privacy, security, or information-reliability concern.

## Names of controls

Bold text such as **Search**, **Subscribe**, or **Print Agenda Only** identifies a label or control on the screen.

## Screenshots

Screenshots may contain sample district names, meetings, dates, or content. Your district's information will differ, but the relevant controls and workflow should be similar.

## Phones and tablets

1. Select **☰ Topics** to open the guide navigation.
2. Select a page.
3. Select the page-outline icon to open its Table of Contents.
4. Select a heading to move directly to that section.

On larger screens, guide navigation and the page Table of Contents may remain visible.

## Live information

Always use the live district site as the current source. A notification email, printed agenda, or downloaded attachment may have been replaced or revised.

## Questions and accessibility

Contact the district when meeting information is unclear. Use [Accessibility and help](accessibility.md) to report a barrier or request an alternative format.
