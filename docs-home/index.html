What is schoolboard.net?

schoolboard.net is an online meeting and agenda platform for school districts and other public organizations. It provides one place to publish meeting notices, agendas, supporting materials, and related public information.

The platform is designed with accessibility in mind and supports WCAG 2.1 Level AA practices, including keyboard navigation, screen-reader-friendly structure, responsive pages, and accessible HTML content. Districts can provide public information while limiting confidential Board materials to authorized, signed-in users.
