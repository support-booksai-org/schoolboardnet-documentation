# Group Administrator FAQ

## I cannot find an agenda in the group

First check **Drafts & Templates**. The agenda may still be unpublished.

Also confirm that the agenda was assigned to the correct group. Content assigned to another group will not appear where expected.

If the agenda still cannot be found, ask a District Administrator to search the site-wide **Content** list.

## I see several entries with the same agenda title

The site may be displaying revisions. Review the **Updated** date and time and open the most recent entry.

Do not delete older entries until you have confirmed which entry is the current published agenda.

## Should I create an agenda from Add Content or clone a template?

Clone a template from **Drafts & Templates** whenever a suitable template exists. Use **Add Content** only when the agenda must be created from scratch.

## Why is my cloned agenda missing from the group?

Open the agenda and verify its Group assignment. During cloning, click the intended **Group** even if it appears highlighted. A highlighted Group is not necessarily selected.

## Why is a published agenda missing from the public group page?

Check all of the following:

- **Visibility** is set to **Public**.
- The agenda is **Published**.
- The correct **Group** is assigned.
- The meeting date and other required fields are complete.

## How do I notify only group members about a revision?

Follow [Notify group members without notifying the public](notifications-revisions.md#notify-group-members-without-notifying-the-public).

## What does Preview show?

Preview shows the signed-in Group Administrator view. It is not a public preview. Work in Draft Mode, then sign out after publication to verify the anonymous public view.

## How can I confirm public notifications were sent?

Subscribe to public notifications using a monitored personal or independent email address. If the expected notice does not arrive, verify publication, notification settings, and email delivery.

## Can several administrators edit one agenda?

They may have permission to edit it, but one person should own the agenda preparation process. schoolboard.net locks an agenda while one administrator is editing it. If a warning identifies another editor, contact that person and wait for the agenda to be saved or unlocked. See [Edit locks and safe agenda editing](edit-locks.md).

If the lock remains after the named editor has stopped editing, contact a District Administrator. District Administrators can release a confirmed stuck lock after verifying that no unsaved work needs to be preserved.

## What should I do when I am unsure?

Stop and ask. There are no stupid questions. Contact the district's primary administrator or schoolboard.net support before making a change that might affect publication, visibility, notifications, or agenda structure.
