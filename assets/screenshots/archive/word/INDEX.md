## Folder Rules

- Do not use spaces in screenshot filenames.
- Use lowercase words separated by hyphens in the description portion.
- Keep original captures in `raw`.
- Put annotated copies in `annotated`.
- Put final publication copies in `approved`.
- Move outdated screenshots to `archive`.
- Do not store screenshot metadata in separate image-specific `.md` files.
