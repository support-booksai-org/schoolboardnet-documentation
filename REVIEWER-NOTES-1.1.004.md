# schoolboard.net Documentation Suite — v1.1.004 Review Edition

## Current state

The core documentation suite is complete for reviewer evaluation:

- Board Member Guide
- Public User Guide
- Group Administrator Guide
- District Administrator Guide
- Accordion Agenda Guide

The District Administrator Guide now begins with a responsibilities overview covering first-line district support, user and Group access, content oversight, security-controlled requests, and the distinction between District and Group Administrator authority.

The Accordion Agenda Guide includes task-focused procedures, troubleshooting, an Accordion Agenda Quick Start, and a Final Publication Checklist. Long pages were divided while their former URLs were retained as overview pages.

## Reviewer priorities

Please identify:

- inaccurate, incomplete, or incorrectly ordered steps;
- terminology that differs from the application;
- unclear screenshot annotations or explanations;
- privacy, security, notification, or revision guidance requiring clarification;
- pages that remain too long or duplicate another page;
- broken links, missing images, mobile-navigation problems, or accessibility concerns; and
- important operational practices that are still missing.

When possible, include the guide, page title, heading, screenshot ID, device, expected result, and recommended correction.
